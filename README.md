# Analytics Case Studies

Applied statistics and data analysis by Kai Marsh, BCom (Finance & Statistics) and Diploma of Mathematical Sciences (Statistics), University of Melbourne. Each folder is a self-contained analysis with a one-page summary (its README) and a full report (PDF/HTML).

| # | Case study | Question | Methods | Output |
|---|-----------|----------|---------|--------|
| 01 | [Energy emissions dashboard](01-energy-emissions-dashboard/) | What 2050 electricity mix gets Australia to net zero? | R Shiny, sf, scenario modelling | [Live demo](https://z0q49p-kai-marsh.shinyapps.io/energy_app/) |
| 02 | [Gallstone disease prediction](02-gallstone-disease-prediction/) | Can routine blood tests flag gallstone patients before imaging? | Logistic regression, random forest, XGBoost | [PDF](02-gallstone-disease-prediction/report.pdf) · [Read online](https://kyanite03.github.io/analytics-case-studies/02-gallstone-disease-prediction/report.html) |
| 03 | [Coral trout reef monitoring](03-coral-trout-reef-monitoring/) | Do no-take zones hold more fish, and can lost reef records be rebuilt? | Poisson / quasi-Poisson GLM, mixture models, EM | [PDF](03-coral-trout-reef-monitoring/report.pdf) · [Read online](https://kyanite03.github.io/analytics-case-studies/03-coral-trout-reef-monitoring/report.html) |
| 04 | [Equity return clustering](04-equity-return-clustering/) | How much of Dow Jones co-movement is one market factor? | PCA / eigen-analysis, k-means | [PDF](04-equity-return-clustering/report.pdf) · [Read online](https://kyanite03.github.io/analytics-case-studies/04-equity-return-clustering/report.html) |
| 05 | [Likelihood & GLM casework](05-glm-likelihood-casework/) | Titanic survival, penguin odds ratios, gamma GLM theory | Probit MLE from first principles, logistic GLM | [PDF](05-glm-likelihood-casework/report.pdf) · [Read online](https://kyanite03.github.io/analytics-case-studies/05-glm-likelihood-casework/report.html) |
| 06 | [Bayesian inference methods](06-bayesian-inference-methods/) | Do exact, MCMC and variational posteriors agree? | Gibbs, Metropolis–Hastings, CAVI | [PDF](06-bayesian-inference-methods/report.pdf) · [Read online](https://kyanite03.github.io/analytics-case-studies/06-bayesian-inference-methods/report.html) |

The work began as university projects (University of Melbourne and RMIT, 2025–26) and has been rewritten as independent analyses. Client scenarios are illustrative. Data sources and licences are listed in each folder's `data/README.md`.
